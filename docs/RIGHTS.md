# Rights boundaries

OldUnreal's [license notice](https://github.com/OldUnreal/UnrealTournamentPatches/blob/master/LICENSE.md) refers UT use and distribution to Epic's terms and lists third-party licenses. That is not a grant to redistribute an Android-transformed engine. Obtain and document applicable permission before distributing any runtime or derivative. The archive stays ignored and is used here only for the requested local inspection.

UTP's [rights notice](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/utp/blob/main/RIGHTS_AND_LICENSES.md) states there is no general top-level license grant for its project-owned code. No source from that project was intentionally copied; file-level provenance still needs review. Study of design ideas is distinct from permission to reuse code. Game content must be supplied by the user; the app imports a selected folder into its own storage. Distribution-specific rights remain unresolved.
